Priscila Santos is an Information Systems student at the Federal University
of the Recôncavo da Bahia and a Front-End AI Engineering Intern at FlyRank AI.

She builds web applications using React, TypeScript, and Java, using AI as a
development partner for planning, implementation, debugging, testing, and
documentation.

## Background

Currently studying Information Systems while working as a Front-End AI
Engineering Intern at FlyRank AI, combining coursework with real internship
deliverables — including her portfolio itself.

## How she works with AI

She treats AI as a collaborator, not a replacement for engineering judgment.
In practice: planning architecture before generating code, reviewing every AI
suggestion before it lands in a project, refining prompts iteratively rather
than accepting the first output, and keeping the final implementation
decisions hers.

## Technologies

React, TypeScript, Next.js, Tailwind CSS, JavaScript, HTML/CSS, Java (Spring

## Values

Understand a tool well enough to explain it before shipping it. Validate AI
output instead of trusting it by default. Write documentation that makes
